## Statement of Purpose lines (3–4)
In my real-estate quant project, I moved beyond static forecasting to a full capital-allocation framework that links valuation quality, financing structure, and downside risk. I implemented walk-forward validation and correlated regime simulation so investment recommendations reflect macro uncertainty rather than point estimates. This process taught me how model design changes real decisions—especially when distinguishing attractive expected returns from unacceptable tail-risk exposure. At MIT, I want to extend this approach into multi-asset portfolio and private-market decision systems.
